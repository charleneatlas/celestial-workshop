Celestial Workshop · navigation icons

Twelve original single-colour engravings for the ISDK button Icon slot (24 × 24 units = 12 mm at canvas scale 0.0005; about 9.6 mm on the title row at scale 0.8). Line weight 2.0 units in the 24 slot, details 1.5 to 1.6, round caps. White PNGs tint to ink (0.22, 0.12, 0.05) through the WorkshopButton animator clips, and to cream on press.

Each card: large view, small sizes, the glyph on parchment / wood / brass, a pixel-enlarged headset-resolution simulation, and a mock workshop button (normal and pressed).
